# HL_UEVR
A mod for UEVR when playing Hogwarts Legacy

## Features include:
- Full first person 6DOF motion controls
- Optional IK arms or visible hands
- Full body support
- Optional gesture based Spell casting (using Glyph System)
- Optional built in low latency voice casting
- Smooth Cutscene transitions
- Physically controlled mounts
- Controller based mouse movement in menus
- Optional HUD widgets attached to hands
- Boats can be ridden in and controlled
- 3D Interactive Gadget Wheel
- In game tutorial

## Guide:
### Settings
Setting for the mod can be accessed by pressing L3+R3 (or INS on your keyboard) and selecting the Hogwarts Legacy Config tab in the lower left column of the UEVR screen

### Gadget Wheel
The Gadget Wheel is where your potions and mounts can be accessed. Open the wheel by holding out your left hand and tapping the left trigger. You will then see all of your potions and combat plants in a circle around your hand. Grab one of the items with your left hand. To use a potion, bring it to your mouth. To use an plant, toss your hand forward while releasing the grip. You can also just release a grabbed item and it wont be consumed but will become your active item. See the video below for a tutorial on how to use the Gadget Wheel. If you prefer the standard Gadget Wheel that the game provides, set "Potion Wheel" to "2D Native" in the Hogwarts Legacy Config settings.

### Spells
Spells can optionally be cast by drawing glyphs or speaking the spell. Spells can be cast this way at any time (as long as you have already learned the spell) They do not need to be assigned to one of the 4 active spell slots
#### Glyph Gesture Casting
The mod features a gesture based glyph drawing system that will recognize the glyphs you draw with your wand. See this video for a demo on the gestures that can be drawn
<BR><BR>
[![Watch Video](https://img.youtube.com/vi/cQr5az_GCbA/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cQr5az_GCbA)
<BR><BR>
To draw a glyph, hold down the left trigger and start drawing. When you are finished drawing, release the left trigger. If the glyph is recognized, the spell will be cast and the symbol of the glyph will appear on the back of your right hand. A symbol on the back of your right hand tells you that spell can be cast again just by flicking your wrist down. For spells like Lumos (or Accio when pulling something), flicking your wrist up instead of down will deactivate the spell
#### Voice Casting
You can also cast spells by speaking the spell word. While most spell words correspond to the name of the spell in the Spells screen, there are some spells that do not have spell words in the original game. Here is a list of the spells that did not have spell words and the spell words you can use to cast them

- Conjuration - Fabricaste
- Alteration - Mutatio
- Vanishment - Evanesco
- Capture creatures - Sanctuarium
- Feed creatures - Nutritio
- Pet creatures - Placato
- Disillusionment - Obscura
- Magic compass trails - Viaspecto
- Ancient Magic Throw - Oppugno
- Ancient Magic - Tormentum

The spell words can also be found in the Spell screen in the game. Hover over a spell in the Spell Screen and in addition to the Glyph, underneath you will see the spell word that can be spoken.

### Mounts
In addition to the normal way to summon mounts, you can also summon mounts by voice command (if it is enabled). Here are the commands you can say to summon your mounts
- Broom - Upbroom
- Hippogriff - Uphippogriff
- Graphorn - Upgraphorn
  
Once you are on your mount, you can optionally grab you mount with you left hand, right hand or both hands. If either or both hands are grabbing the mount you can then physically control the direction the mount is flying. Tilt your hands forward or back to make the mount go up and down (except for Graphorn of course) and tilt your hand left or right to make the mount go left or right. ### Boats
Have you ever been down at the boathouse or the docks and said "Why can't I ride in one of those boats?" Now you can. Just say "Navigato" when you are close to a body of water to apparate your boat. When you are on or near the boat, press X to control it. Press X again to release control. No need to put the boat away. Just leave it where it is and it will come to you the next time your say "Navigato". Watch a demo in the video below

### Custom Gestures
- Cast Protego - Block with your left hand (hold your left hand up in front of you with palm away from you). Hold longer for Stupefy
- Heal - Left grip near mouth
- Open Inventory - Left grip near your ear
- Jump - Right Stick forward
- Dodge - Right stick back + Left Stick direction

### HUD
The HUD widgets can be placed on your hands instead of the HUD for better immersion in the game. In the game settings, check the box that says "Enable Hand Placement". The HUD items will then be attached to your hands at default locations. If you are happy with their placement then that's all you need to do. You can also adjust the placement of each item by opening the tree for that item. Select "UI" for "Attach To" if you want that widget on the HUD. Select "None" if you don't want to see the widget at all. Otherwise, you can select the hand that the widget will attach to and adjust its location, rotation and scale.
