
# HL_UEVR
A mod for UEVR when playing Hogwarts Legacy

## Features include:

## Features include:
- Full first person 6DOF motion controls
- Optional IK arms or visible hands
- Full body support
- Optional gesture based Spell casting (using Glyph System)
- Optional built in low latency voice casting
- Smooth Cutscene transitions
- Physically controlled mounts
- Controller based mouse movement in menus

## Guide:
### Spells
Spells can optionally be cast by drawing glyphs or speaking the spell. Spells can be cast this way at any time (as long as you have already learned the spell) They do not need to be assigned to one of the 4 active spell slots
#### Glyph Gesture Casting
The mod features a gesture based glyph drawing system that will recognize the glyphs you draw with your wand. See this video for a demo on the gestures that can be drawn
<BR><BR>
[![Watch Video](https://img.youtube.com/vi/cQr5az_GCbA/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cQr5az_GCbA)
<BR><BR>
To draw a glyph, hold down the left trigger and start drawing. When you are finished drawing, release the left trigger. If the glyph is recognized, the spell will be cast and the symbol of the glyph will appear on the back of your right hand. A symbol on the back of your right hand tells you that spell can be cast again just by flicking your wrist down. For spells like Lumos (or Accio when pulling something), flicking your wrist up instead of down will deactivate the spell
#### Voice Casting
You can also cast spells by speaking the spell word. While most spell words correspond to the name of the spell in the Spells screen, there are some spells that do not have spell words in the original game. Here is a list of the spells that did not have spell words and the spell words you can use to cast them

- Conjuration - Fabricaste
- Alteration - Mutatio
- Vanishment - Evanesco
- Capture creatures - Sanctuarium
- Feed creatures - Nutritio
- Pet creatures - Placato
- Disillusionment - Obscura
- Magic compass trails - Viaspecto
- Ancient Magic Throw - Oppugno
- Ancient Magic - Tormentum

The spell words can also be found in the Spell screen in the game. Hover over a spell in the Spell Screen and in addition to the Glyph, underneath you will see the spell word that can be spoken.

### Mounts
In addition to the normal way to summon mounts, you can also summon mounts by voice command (if it is enabled). Here are the commands you can say to summon your mounts
- Broom - Upbroom
- Hippogriff - Uphippogriff
- Graphorn - Upgraphorn
  
Once you are on your mount, you can optionally grab you mount with you left hand, right hand or both hands. If either or both hands are grabbing the mount you can then physically control the direction the mount is flying. Tilt your hands forward or back to make the mount go up and down (except for Graphorn of course) and tilt your hand left or right to make the mount go left or right. See the video below for a demonstration

### Custom Gestures
- Cast Protego - Block with your left hand (hold your left hand up in front of you with palm away from you). Hold longer for Stupefy
- Heal - Left grip near mouth
- Open Inventory - Left grip near your ear
